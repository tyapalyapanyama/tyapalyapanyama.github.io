Ranking and credit requirements for problem classes:

To receive credit for the problem classes, you must meet the following requirements:

1) Take two problem-solving tests. The first test is held in the middle of the semester and the second at the end. There will be one retake of each test during the semester and another retake on the credit assessment day. Problem-solving tests are passed problem by problem.
2) Take a derivatives quiz or complete 50 differentiation problems.
3) Take a theory test. This test is assessed as a whole.
4) Earn at least 60% of the points for the basic (non-ranking) problems in the problem sheets.

The ranking in mathematical analysis is based on both ranking and basic problems from the problem sheets. There will be three problem sheets in total. Solutions cannot be submitted after the deadline stated on the sheet.

If N students solve a ranking problem, each receives ⌈30⋅exp(−N2/239)⌉ points for it; here ⌈⋅⌉ denotes the ceiling function. Points for individual problems are added using ordinary addition. If a ranking problem has several parts, each part is scored separately.

If S is the total number of points for ranking problems and Σ is the total number of points for basic problems, the student receives 80%⋅S+20%⋅Σ points for the problem sheets. Students are ranked according to the resulting scores. The cutoffs in this course ranking are determined jointly by the course instructors.
