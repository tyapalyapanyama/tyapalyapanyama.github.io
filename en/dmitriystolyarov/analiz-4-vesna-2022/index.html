Pass requirements:

To pass analysis, you must:

  1. Take two practical tests. The first test takes place in the middle of the semester (at the end of March), and the second at the end. There will be one retake of each test during the semester, as well as one retake on the day of the final pass/fail assessment. Credit for the practical tests is awarded problem by problem.
  2. Earn at least 50% of the points for the basic (non-ranking) problems on the problem sheets. There will be 2 sheets in total (one in March and one in April). The MAAD groups will receive only the first sheet.
  3. Complete the required homework (3 integrals and 3 conformal mappings). It will be assigned at the end of April. If an error is found or a solution is not submitted on time, the number of integrals required to pass is doubled.

The ranking score for mathematical analysis is based on both the ranking problems and the basic problems on the problem sheets. There will be two problem sheets in total. Solutions cannot be submitted after the deadline specified on the sheet.
