Course credit requirements:

To receive course credit for mathematical analysis, you must solve all
problems on every test, submit the homework assignments “25 Derivatives” and “13
Antiderivatives”, and solve at least two problems from each sheet of
theoretical problems for the lecture course.

Rating calculation rules:

70 rating points are awarded for solving the rating section of the problem sheet.
Student S's rating is calculated using the formula \Sigma_S/\Sigma_{Max}, where
\Sigma_{S} is the total value of the problems solved by S, and \Sigma_{\Max} is
the maximum value of \Sigma_S over all S. The value of problem Z is calculated using the
formula N_{all} + K_Z\cdot N_{UnSolve}, where N_\all is the total number of
students, N_{UnSolve} is the number of students who did not solve Z, and K_Z \in
[1,3] is the individual coefficient for the problem (set in advance).
Non-rating problems on the sheet earn no points!

The remaining 30 points are awarded for four homework assignments and two tests.
