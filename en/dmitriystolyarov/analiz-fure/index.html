Course credit requirements:

To receive course credit for analysis, you must:

  1. Take two problem-solving tests. The first test is held in the middle of the semester (at the end of October), and the second at the end. There will be one retake of each test during the semester, and one additional retake on the course assessment day. Credit for the problem-solving tests is awarded problem by problem.
  2. Earn at least 50% of the points for the basic (non-rating) problems on the problem sheets. There will be two sheets in total (one at the end of September and one at the beginning of November). MAAD groups receive only the first sheet.
  3. Complete the required homework (one exercise on Laplace's method and one on the method of stationary phase). It will be assigned in mid-November.

The rating for mathematical analysis is based on both the rating problems and the basic problems on the sheets. There will be two problem sheets in total. Solutions cannot be submitted after the deadline stated on the sheet.
