General rules for problem sheets:

  • Solutions submitted before the deadline receive full credit; those submitted afterward receive only half credit. A solution review session takes place one week after the deadline, after which no further submissions are accepted
  • Solutions are presented orally to one of the assessors; in exceptional circumstances (for example, illness), written solutions may be submitted by email in TEX format
  • Anyone who has solved all the problems on a sheet may receive and submit additional problems
  • Solution presentations take place OUTSIDE the Chebyshev Laboratory premises
