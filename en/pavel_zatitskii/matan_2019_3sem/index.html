General rules:

  • Solutions submitted before the deadline are eligible for full credit. After the deadline, the points awarded for problems are halved.
  • Solutions are presented orally to one of the people accepting them. In exceptional cases (for example, due to illness), written solutions may be submitted by email in PDF format (compiled from TEX).
  • Students who solve all the problems on a sheet before the deadline may receive and submit additional problems.
  • On each sheet, some problems may be presented only to instructors.
  • Each student takes the theory test once, with no retakes.
  • Solutions are accepted OUTSIDE the Chebyshev Laboratory premises.
  • Rating points are awarded starting at 60 points.
    If fewer than 60 points are earned, the rating is 0.
