Course credit requirements:

To receive credit for mathematical analysis, you must solve all
problems in all tests, submit the homework assignment “30
Antiderivatives”, and solve at least 2 problems from each sheet of
theoretical problems based on the lecture course.


Ranking score rules:

70 ranking points are awarded for solving the ranking section of the problem sheet.
The ranking score of student S is calculated as \Sigma_S/\Sigma_{Max}, where
\Sigma_{S} is the total value of the problems solved by S, and \Sigma_{\Max} is
the maximum of \Sigma_S over all S. The value of problem Z is calculated using
the formula N_{all} + K_Z\cdot N_{UnSolve}, where N_\all is the total number of
students, N_{UnSolve} is the number of students who did not solve Z, and K_Z \in
[1,3] is the individual coefficient for the problem (set in advance).
The non-ranking problems on the sheet earn no points!

The remaining 30 points are awarded for 4 homework assignments and 2 tests.
