Course credit requirements:

To receive credit for analysis, you must:

  1. Take two problem-solving tests. The first test is held in the middle of the semester (at the end of October), and the second at the end of the semester. There will be one retake of each test during the semester and another retake on the credit assessment day. Problem-solving tests are passed problem by problem. MAAD groups only need to take the first test
  2. Take a theory test (at the end of October). This test is assessed as a whole and cannot be retaken.
  3. Earn at least 50% of the points for the basic (non-ranking) problems in the problem sheets. There will be 3 sheets in total (one in September, one in October, and one in November). MAAD groups will receive only the first two sheets.
  4. Complete the compulsory homework (6 integrals). It will be assigned at the end of November (and does not apply to MAAD groups). If an error is found or a solution is not submitted on time, the number of integrals required for credit doubles.

The ranking in mathematical analysis is based on both ranking and basic problems from the problem sheets. There will be three problem sheets in total. Solutions cannot be submitted after the deadline stated on the sheet.
