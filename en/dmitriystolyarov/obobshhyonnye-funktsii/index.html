Pass/fail assessment and examination requirements. To pass the assessment, you must earn 10 points by solving exercises. You may submit solutions written in tex, or come to class and present them at the board to the other participants. Each time, the student with the fewest points among those who have solved the exercise but have not submitted their solutions will be called to the board (if everyone who solved it has submitted a solution, one of them will be chosen).

You must also pass an examination. Those taking the course with reduced requirements may use their lecture notes. A bonus system is available for everyone else.

30 exercise points allow you to use lecture notes during the examination

40 points earn an excellent grade without having to solve a problem

50 points earn an automatic excellent grade

In addition, preparing notes for one class in tex earns 5 points (but these are credited only after you have earned 10 exercise points).
