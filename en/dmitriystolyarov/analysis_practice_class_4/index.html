General rules for problem sheets:

  • Solutions submitted before the deadline receive full credit; solutions submitted after it receive only half credit. A solutions review takes place one week after the deadline, after which no further submissions are accepted
  • Solutions are presented orally to one of the people accepting them. In exceptional cases (for example, due to illness), written solutions may be submitted by email in TEX format
  • Students who solve every problem on a sheet may receive and submit additional problems
  • Solutions are accepted OUTSIDE the Chebyshev Laboratory premises
