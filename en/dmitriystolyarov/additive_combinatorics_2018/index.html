Exam topics for 2018:

1. Equality cases in the trivial bounds for the cardinalities of A+B and A-B;
Ruzsa distance; when the doubling constant and the difference constant equal one.

2. Petridis’s lemma and the proof of Plünnecke’s inequality.

3. Bounds for the difference and doubling constants in terms of one another; Ruzsa’s covering lemma; a structural description of sets with a small doubling constant or a small difference constant.

4. Additive energy, its relation to the doubling and difference constants, and its expression in terms of the Fourier transform; the Bogolyubov–Ruzsa theorem.

5. A bound for the exponential moment of a trigonometric polynomial with frequencies in a dissociated set.

6. A bound for the size of a dissociated subset of a superlevel set of the Fourier transform, and completion of the proof of Theorem 1.2.5.

7. Lattice theory up to Minkowski’s second theorem.

8. Minkowski’s second theorem.

9. Derivation of Theorem 1.2.6 from Minkowski’s second theorem

10. Freiman homomorphisms and a proof of the existence of a large arithmetic progression in 2A-2A.

11. Chang’s covering lemma and completion of the proof of Freiman’s theorem.

12. Szemerédi’s regularity lemma.

13. The counting lemma; proof of the triangle removal lemma; the case k=3 of Szemerédi’s theorem.

14. Statement of the hypergraph removal lemma and derivation of Szemerédi’s theorem from it.

15. The hypergraph regularity lemma (overview).

16. Statement of the hypergraph counting lemma and derivation of the hypergraph removal lemma (overview).

17. A Besicovitch set in the plane.

18. The Katz–Tao 4/7 theorem.

19. The Kakeya conjecture over finite fields.
