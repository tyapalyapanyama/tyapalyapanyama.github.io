Ranking and credit requirements for problem classes:

To receive credit for the problem classes, you must meet the following requirements:

To receive credit for analysis, you must:

 

  1. Solve and submit written solutions to 30 integrals from Sadovnichy’s problem book. The integrals must be evenly distributed over problems No. 161 to No. 400 (in the 1988 edition).
  2. Take two problem-solving tests. The first test is held in the middle of the semester and the second at the end. There will be one retake of each test during the semester and another retake on the credit assessment day. Problem-solving tests are passed problem by problem.
  3. Take a theory test. This test is assessed as a whole. 
  4. Earn at least 60% of the points for the basic (non-ranking) problems in the problem sheets. There will be two more sheets.

 

Integrals: If more than four errors are found in the integral assignment, the student is assigned min(10+4*(number of errors — 4), 30) penalty integrals. Each error in the penalty integrals results in another 5 penalty integrals; errors in these and subsequent assignments also result in 5 penalty integrals each (with no more than 30 integrals in each penalty assignment).

The deadline for the integral assignment is 12 April at 23:59.

The ranking in mathematical analysis is based on both ranking and basic problems from the problem sheets. There will be three problem sheets in total. Solutions cannot be submitted after the deadline stated on the sheet.

 

If N students solve a ranking problem, each receives points for it; here denotes the ceiling function. Points for individual problems are added using ordinary addition. If a ranking problem has several parts, each part is scored separately.
